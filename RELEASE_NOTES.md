Illustrated MCAT covers all seven books and 116 chapters.

- 7,227 notes generating 7,245 cards
- 1,270 textbook figures and five bundled fonts
- Book subdecks and chapter tags
- Practice links on card backs

## Installation

Download `illustrated-mcat-1.0.0.apkg` and import it into Anki. Back up your collection before updating an existing installation.

The deck works offline. Linked practice requires internet and may require an account or paid access.
