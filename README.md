# Illustrated MCAT — an Anki deck

A free MCAT flashcard deck drawn in the same hand as the *Illustrated MCAT*
book series from [MedBrewery](https://www.medbrewery.ca). Every figure on a
card is the figure from the book: hand-drawn energy diagrams, pathways,
molecules, circuits, brain maps and plots, with the book's one-line margin
mnemonics turned into cloze cards and its exercises turned into worked
problems.

**[Download on AnkiWeb](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/1750297190)** ·
[GitHub releases](https://github.com/medbrewery/illustrated-mcat/releases)

See [release validation](docs/release-validation.md) for automated checks and owner-reported testing.

## What is in it

| Included in 1.0.0 | Count |
|---|---:|
| Accepted notes | 7,227 |
| Study cards | 7,245 |
| Packaged textbook figures | 1,270 |
| Books / chapter tags | 7 / 116 |
| Bundled fonts | 5 |

There is one subdeck per book; chapters are tags, not additional subdecks.

The seven books are Physics and Math, General Chemistry, Organic Chemistry,
Biology, Biochemistry, Critical Reading (CARS), and Psychology and Sociology.
Every scientific claim on a card is checkable against the printed book it
came from, and each card carries that reference.

## Install

1. Install [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) on desktop.
2. Download the deck from [AnkiWeb](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/1750297190)
   or get a specific version from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/medbrewery/illustrated-mcat/releases).
3. In Anki: **File → Import**, choose the file, keep "Update existing notes"
   on. Updating to a later version the same way replaces changed cards and
   is designed to preserve your review history. Back up your collection before updating.
4. Sync to AnkiWeb to study on mobile. The hand-lettered fonts travel inside
   the deck, for offline use. Rendering can vary between clients.

See [docs/card-guide.md](docs/card-guide.md) for how the deck is organised,
what the tags mean, and a suggested study plan.

Practice links on card backs open MedBrewery in your browser. The deck works offline; linked practice requires internet and may require an account or paid access.

## Who it is for

Pre-med students preparing for the MCAT who learn better from a drawing than
from a wall of text. The deck includes AAMC content tags where applicable and is meant
to be studied alongside a content review, not instead of one: a card tests
one fact, and the book or lesson it points to teaches the surrounding idea.

## Found a mistake?

Open a [card error report](https://github.com/medbrewery/illustrated-mcat/issues/new?template=card-error.md) with the
card ID printed at the bottom of the card. Confirmed errors are listed in
[docs/errata.md](docs/errata.md) until the next release fixes them. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for why fixes arrive as releases rather
than pull requests.
